Classic-X Menu has been successfully installed!

The Classic-X Kicker applet is now ready for use on your Trinity desktop.

How to add Classic-X to your panel:

  1. Right-click on an empty area of your desktop panel (Kicker).
  2. Select Add Applet to Panel...
  3. Choose Classic-X Menu from the list and click Add to Panel.
  4. Right-click on the Classic-X Start button to open Classic-X Settings and customize your themes and profiles.

Tip: You can move or replace the default KMenu by unlocking the panel buttons.
